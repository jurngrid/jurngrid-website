Why JurnGrid

The information already exists. The context is what gets lost.

Cloud teams work across accounts, services, logs, alerts, tickets and dashboards. The challenge is often not a lack of data, but having enough connected context to understand what changed and why it matters.

JurnGrid is being built around that gap: a calm, continuous operational record that helps teams look back, understand the present and respond with better context.

The goal is not another noisy console. It is a clearer way to understand the environment as it changes.

The name

A living journal for an interconnected environment.

JurnGrid is built from two simple ideas: a record that keeps moving, and the infrastructure landscape it moves through.

Jurn

A living journal — an ongoing record of activity, change and operational history.

Grid

The connected environment — resources, services, relationships and layers that make up cloud infrastructure.

Current stage

AWS-first. Early build. Designed to grow with the problem.

JurnGrid is currently in development, beginning with AWS infrastructure visibility and change tracking. The longer-term direction expands into broader operational monitoring, security context, reporting and deployment models that can support cloud and customer-managed environments.
